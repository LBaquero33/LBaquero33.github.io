Pitch-flight analysis / player development

Butler Pitch Tunnel

Reconstructs individual TrackMan trajectories to find pitch pairs that hold the same visual lane early and separate after the hitter must decide.

This Butler slider–fastball pair starts from nearly the same release and finishes 18.3 inches apart.

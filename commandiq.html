Computer vision / pitch review

CommandIQ

A bullpen command workflow that uses the catcher’s glove flash as the intended target and compares it with the pitch result.

Built with
Swift, Python, Supabase
Data
TrackMan and Rapsodo-style CSVs
Focus
Computer vision and command review

The project

The glove flash sets the target.

CommandIQ looks for the catcher’s glove flash before the pitch and treats that spot as the intended target.

It then compares the target with where the pitch crossed the plate, pairing video with TrackMan or Rapsodo-style data.

Coaches confirm the target, review pitches one by one, save sessions, and export the results.

Workflow

Built around the review process.

The product keeps pitch data, intended location, video evidence, and saved results in one continuous workflow.

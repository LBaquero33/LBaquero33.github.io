04 · Computer vision

CommandIQ

Swift · Python · Supabase

Command is the gap between where a pitch was supposed to go and where it went. CommandIQ draws the intended target on video so you can see that gap.

Built with
Swift, Python, Supabase
Pitch data
TrackMan and Rapsodo exports
Output
Pitch-by-pitch review, saved sessions, report export

The review frame

See the target. Review the miss.

The catcher’s glove before the pitch is the best public clue to where the pitch was meant to go. CommandIQ keeps that mark next to the measured result for every pitch in a bullpen.

Workflow

The coach always has the last word

  • ImportReads pitch type, velocity and location from TrackMan or Rapsodo exports.
  • Mark the targetClick the glove on the video, or start from a computer-vision suggestion.
  • ConfirmNothing is applied until the coach confirms the target and the strike-zone calibration.
  • ReviewCompare the confirmed target with where the pitch finished, then export the session as a report.

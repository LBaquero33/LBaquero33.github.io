CommandIQ

Computer vision for intended-location review

CommandIQ draws the intended target on video, so you can see where a pitch was supposed to go and how far it missed.

It covers a full bullpen in one place: the video, target entry and pitch-by-pitch review.

The user confirms each target, and the session exports as a report.

How it works

  • ImportReads pitch type, velocity and location from TrackMan or Rapsodo-style exports.
  • Mark the targetClick the catcher’s glove on the video, or start from a computer-vision suggestion.
  • ConfirmNothing is applied until the coach confirms the target and the strike-zone calibration.
  • ReviewCompare the confirmed target with where the pitch finished.

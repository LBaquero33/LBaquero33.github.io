03 · Single-camera motion tracking

CommandIQ
Motion Lab

Butler BaseballPython · R · TrackMan

Point one camera at a bullpen. Motion Lab tracks the pitcher’s joints through the delivery, builds a 3D estimate, and lines it up with the TrackMan data for the same pitch.

Input
Video from one fixed camera, plus a TrackMan export
Tracking
Up to 33 body points per frame
Output
A replay you can scrub and a coach report

Camera mapping

Arm, hips and feet, frame by frame

The model draws a skeleton over the pitcher in every frame, following the throwing arm from the glove break through release.

The counter in the corner shows how many of the 33 points it could see in that frame, so a reviewer knows when to trust it.

Checkpoints

Four moments, confirmed by a person

  • Leg liftThe peak of the front knee.
  • Foot contactThe front foot lands and the arm is loaded.
  • ReleaseThe ball leaves the hand.
  • Follow-throughThe arm finishes across the body.

The model proposes each one as a candidate. Nothing counts until a reviewer confirms it.

Pitch replay

Video, motion and ball flight on one playhead

What it is and isn’t. The 3D motion is an estimate from one camera. It isn’t motion capture or a medical measurement, and TrackMan numbers attach only after someone reviews the match.

Workflow

From a phone video to a coach report

  • 1. ImportBring in a recording from a fixed camera and the TrackMan file from the same session.
  • 2. MatchConfirm which TrackMan pitch belongs to which clip.
  • 3. ReviewCheck the four checkpoints on the timeline.
  • 4. ExportSave a replay and a coach report.

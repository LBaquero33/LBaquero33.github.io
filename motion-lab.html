CommandIQ Motion Lab

Turns video from one camera into a 3D estimate of a pitcher’s delivery, lined up with the TrackMan data for the same pitch

Joint tracking on the camera view

Pitch replay

The replay and a coach report both export from the session.

The 3D motion is an estimate from one camera. It isn’t motion capture or a medical measurement, and TrackMan numbers are attached only after someone reviews the match.

How it works

  • 1. ImportBring in a recording from a fixed camera.
  • 2. CheckConfirm the pitch match and the event markers: leg lift, foot contact, release, follow-through.
  • 3. CompareLook at the estimated motion next to the measured ball flight.
  • 4. ExportSave a replay and a coach report.

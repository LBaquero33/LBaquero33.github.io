Player Development

R · Shiny · TrackMan

Ball Fox reports for college hitters and pitchers. Each one is built around a question a coach would actually ask.

Open the Shiny app ↗
Built with
R, Shiny, TrackMan exports
For
College players and coaches
Output
Player-level hitter and pitcher reports

Hitter report

What happens with two strikes?

A spray chart, whiff rate by zone, and chase and contact rates, all filtered to two-strike counts.

Under the charts the report answers four questions in plain English: does he fight pitches off, does he swing at strikes, does he chase, does he whiff too much.

Pitcher report

What’s working, and when?

Results by pitch type, the put-away pitch with two strikes, usage when ahead, even and behind, and a command table (zone, edge, waste, chase).

One page a coach can read before a bullpen.

College player development

Base Player Development Dashboard

Hitter and pitcher reporting from TrackMan-exportable data, built around goals and coaching context.

Open Shiny app ↗
Built with
R, Shiny, TrackMan exports
Audience
College players and coaches
Output
Player-level hitter and pitcher reports

Development review

What changed, and what should the player work on next?

I built the dashboard to track hitting and pitching performance over time and to keep individual goals beside the results.

The report is meant to support a coaching conversation. It adds count, usage, command, and two-strike context instead of presenting one undifferentiated stat line.

Hitter and pitcher views use different summaries because their development questions are different.

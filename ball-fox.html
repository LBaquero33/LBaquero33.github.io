Base Player Development Dashboard

The Ball Fox R Shiny app turns TrackMan-exportable data into player-level hitting and pitching reports.

Open the app ↗
Built with
R, Shiny, TrackMan
Audience
College players and coaches
Focus
Results, development goals, and context

The project

Track progress. Explain results.

The dashboard tracks hitting and pitching performance over time and generates reports that players and coaches can use together.

It is built to track goals and add context to the result—not simply list outcomes.

Hitter and pitcher views keep the reporting specific to the decisions each player is working on.

Reports

Two sides of player development.

Hitting and pitching reports organize pitch-level data into a format intended for direct coaching conversations.

01

Track

Follow hitting and pitching progress over time with TrackMan-exportable data.

02

Contextualize

Connect individual results to count, usage, command, and the player’s development goals.

03

Communicate

Give coaches and players a shared report for reviewing progress and planning the next step.

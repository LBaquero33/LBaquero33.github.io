Pitch comparison

Do these two pitches stay together long enough to matter?

The app reconstructs individual pitch trajectories rather than drawing generic pitch shapes.

The behind-the-zone view keeps the release lane, the decision window, and the full strike zone visible while both pitches travel toward the plate.

The pair shown here starts from a similar release area and finishes 18.3 inches apart. That is a description of modeled flight—not proof of hitter deception or effectiveness.

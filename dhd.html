The project

Development work in one place.

The DHD app tracks strength and conditioning and hitting development through role-based coach and player workflows.

Daily logs, calendars, and progress data give athletes a consistent place to record work and give coaches a clearer view of the development process.

The project also includes other management tools built for the program.

01

Player workflow

Log daily hitting and strength work and follow development over time.

02

Coach workflow

Review player activity and progress through a role-specific experience.

03

Support the broader program with additional management tools and shared development context.

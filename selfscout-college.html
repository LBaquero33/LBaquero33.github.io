Recruiting

SelfScout College

A recruiting workspace for a college baseball staff

Recruiting boards, depth charts and scholarship money in one place, so the staff works from the same picture.

Recruiting boards you drag

Each board has its own tiers, and the staff names them: here it’s Priority, Follow and Watch List on the 2027 Pitchers board. Ranking a player is a drag up or down, and changing his tier is a drag across.

Every card carries position, class, where he stands (Offer, Visit, Contacted) and a 1–5 star rating. Players who are unrated, or not on the board yet, wait in the left column so nobody gets lost.

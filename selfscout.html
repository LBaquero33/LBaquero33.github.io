The project

From evaluation to roster plan.

SelfScout brings player information and baseball evaluation into one organized scouting workflow.

Scouts can build hitter and pitcher reports, complete evaluations, organize boards, and carry those decisions into roster planning.

Configurable 20–80 reporting keeps the grading framework adaptable to the way each evaluator works.
